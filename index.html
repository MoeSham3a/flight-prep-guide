Using this form (LIFUS Training & Line Operations Guidance)

• The purpose of this form is to aid in flight preparation during LIFUS training.
• It may be used in electronic or in printed format.
• Use of this form is mandatory for trainees undertaking LIFUS training; it is however accepted that last-minute roster changes may preclude this.
• Completed sections should be discussed with the trainer prior to departure.
• It is worth noting that it may take up to 3 hours to prepare appropriately for a flight to a new destination or through a new area/region.
• Line pilots can use the form should they wish to.
